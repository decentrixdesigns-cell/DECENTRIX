A person behind the pixels

The designer behind DECENTRIX.

John Okeke (daCreator).

JohndaCreator is a brand and graphic designer who helps businesses show up with visuals people remember. He turns what a business stands for into clear, memorable design that tells a story and leaves a lasting impression.

His work spans e-commerce, tech, real estate, and food, giving him experience creating for different audiences and markets. The name says it all: he loves to create, tell stories, and evoke emotions that stay with people.

Clients are never left guessing. He keeps them involved at every stage of the creative process, so the final result reflects their vision.
